a little about me ✿

About

I came to tech looking for a skill I could build alongside nursing school. I tried a few different corners of it, and nothing really clicked until I found software development. Front-end is where it finally made sense.

I’m a very visual person. I love colour, layout, and the way small components come together into something that looks right and feels good to use. Front-end lets me do exactly that, and I get to see the result straight away. Building something and watching it come to life in the browser is my favourite part.

What matters most to me is building things that help people. A website or interface can help a business show what it sells, makes or does, and I want mine to be responsive and accessible, so they work well for everyone who visits.
